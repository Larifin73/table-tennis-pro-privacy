Table Tennis Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

F17B Production ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Table Tennis Pro mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Table Tennis Pro.

1. Information You Enter in the App

Table Tennis Pro allows users to enter information related to table tennis activities, including player names, team names, tournament information, match scores, match results, and related competition information.

This information is primarily used to provide tournament management, scoring, match tracking, standings, reports, and related features.

2. Player Photos

The app may allow users to select or add player photographs from their device for use within the application.

Photos selected by the user are used to provide player identification and presentation features within Table Tennis Pro.

3. Camera and Photo Library

If a feature requires access to photographs, Table Tennis Pro may request permission to access the device's photo library or camera.

Access is requested only when the corresponding feature is used. Users can manage these permissions through their device settings.

4. Microphone and Speech Features

Table Tennis Pro may use microphone access when users choose features that involve speech recognition or voice input.

Microphone access is requested only when the relevant feature is used. The app does not continuously record audio in the background.

5. Live Score and Local Network Features

Table Tennis Pro includes optional live scoring features that can make match information available through a local network connection.

When enabled by the user, information such as player names, scores, match status, and related match information may be displayed through the local live scoring interface.

These live features are intended to operate within the user's local network environment. Users should avoid sharing information they do not have permission to publish.

6. Sharing Features

The application may provide sharing functionality for match or live scoring information. When you choose to share information, the sharing action is controlled by you and may use the sharing facilities provided by your device or other applications you select.

7. Text-to-Speech

Table Tennis Pro may use text-to-speech functionality for match announcements and related scoring features.

8. Local Storage

Table Tennis Pro may store application data locally on your device, including player, team, tournament, match, score, and related application information.

Local application data may remain on the device until it is deleted by the user, removed through application functionality, or removed when the application is uninstalled, depending on the type of data and device operating system behavior.

9. Personal Information

Table Tennis Pro is designed primarily for managing table tennis activities. Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into player names, tournament records, notes, or other fields.

10. Third-Party Services

Table Tennis Pro may use third-party libraries and platform services necessary to provide application functionality, such as operating system services, speech recognition, text-to-speech, file and image selection, sharing, and application distribution services.

Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We do not sell personal information collected or entered through Table Tennis Pro.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Table Tennis Pro is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or profiles.

13. Data Deletion

Users can remove locally stored application information through the application's available data-management features or by removing the application from their device, subject to the behavior of the device operating system.

If you have questions regarding personal information or data associated with Table Tennis Pro, you may contact us using the email address below.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
